About

Tamie Kellogg founded Kellogg Consulting in 1990, beginning as an organizer and facilitator for the community voice in Superfund cleanup sites.

Since then, Kellogg has grown to become a reputable strategic advisor to hundreds of stakeholder engagement and organizational development projects, navigating local, regional and international challenges in both the public and private sector. The past decade, Kellogg's focus has been to help clients in the government and NGO sectors recognize, navigate and proactively address and align with thorny public policy issues and internal organizational challenges in a strategic way.

Principal Tamie Kellogg is sought after for her effectiveness as a neutral facilitator and strategic guide, applying insights gained from over two decades working with State, Federal, Local Government, Tribes, and community-based organizations. She has proven experience in facilitating potentially emotionally charged environments, empowering the community voice, diffusing conflicts and promoting success outcomes.
