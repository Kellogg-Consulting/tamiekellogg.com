Services, Skills & Sectors

Kellogg's focus is to help clients in the government and non-profit sectors recognize, navigate and proactively address thorny public policy issues, as well as tackle the unique challenges in multi-party stakeholder collaboration, public engagement and strategic planning. Under the guidance of principal Tamie Kellogg, Kellogg Consulting has become a go-to consultant for skillful facilitation, strategic initiatives, and public engagement planning.

Kellogg has extensive experience facilitating large public forums, as well as customizing processes for interactive stakeholder participation. Kellogg is particularly adept at designing robust public involvement and communication messaging for complex multi-jurisdictional and cross-sector initiatives. To support this work, Kellogg offers expertise and services in the design and facilitation of focus groups and surveys, outreach tactics for reaching underserved populations, and utilization of social marketing principles to create behavior change.

Kellogg has been applauded for the ability to coalesce common ground and create forward momentum where conflict between competing interests is often a barrier.
